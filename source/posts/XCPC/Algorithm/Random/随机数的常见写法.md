## 声明

**简易写法**

`mt19937 rnd(time(0))`

**32位整数**

`mt19937 rnd((unsigned)chrono::steady_clock::now().time_since_epoch().count())`

**64位整数**

`mt19937_64 rnd64((unsigned)chrono::steady_clock::now().time_since_epoch().count())`
